NOTICE OF DECOMMISSION: VALE MEMORIAL ARCHIVE

The Vale Memorial Archive will move to reduced service from 1 April 2047
and close permanently at 00:00 on 1 November 2049.

Families may claim rememberings and transfer them to approved private
storage before closure. Rememberings not claimed by closure will be
securely deleted. The city regrets that it cannot preserve unclaimed
records indefinitely.
