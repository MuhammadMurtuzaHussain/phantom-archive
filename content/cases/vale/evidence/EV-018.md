NOTES FROM THE NIGHT SHIFT, by Ines Vale
27 October 2049: "Ofrendas"

People ask why I never take the altar down after November. An archive is
an ofrenda that never has to come down. You don't leave marigolds; you
leave your voice, so someone can find you.

The city says that's a library's worth of electricity. The city is wrong.
It's a lot more than that.
